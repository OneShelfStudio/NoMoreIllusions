## Full Description
Step beyond passive listening. **No More Illusions: Album Game** transforms Ethan Correa's progressive electronic LP into an interactive, audio-reactive arcade experience.

Every synth line, bass drop, and drum fill is mapped directly to real-time gameplay mechanics. Navigate your orbital sound-runner through synchronized soundwave tracks, collect frequency crystals, trigger golden overdrive chains, and conquer reactive boss wormholes calibrated to each song's tempo and energy curve.

### Key Features

* **The Complete LP Experience:** Play through album tracks seamlessly with studio-mastered audio stems ("Confide", "Number One").
* **Audio-Reactive Visual Engine:** Live particle fields, dynamic turntable consoles, and responsive shader sweeps pulse in exact sync with every beat.
* **Overdrive & Multipliers:** Time your lane switches and beat taps to build explosive combo chains and climb the global leaderboards.
* **The Artist Vault:** Unlock exclusive high-resolution album artwork, liner notes, lyric insights, and behind-the-scenes production commentary from Ethan Correa.
* **Offline Play Enabled:** Experience the complete interactive album anywhere without data or Wi-Fi.
* **Zero Pay-to-Win:** A pure artistic fusion of electronic music and precision arcade gameplay.

Put on your headphones, tune your frequency, and experience the music like never before.
