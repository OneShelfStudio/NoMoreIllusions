# INTERACTIVE ALBUM CO-DEVELOPMENT & EXCLUSIVITY AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** November 5, 2026  
**Between:**  
1. **One Shelf Studio LLC** ("Studio", "Developer"), and  
2. **Ethan Correa** (p/k/a the "Artist", representing musical works under "No More Illusions")

---

### RECITALS
WHEREAS, Studio has designed, architected, and developed a proprietary mobile and web-based interactive music album application engine known as **"No More Illusions: The Interactive Album"** (the "Platform" / "Game"); and  
WHEREAS, Artist owns and controls the master recordings, underlying musical compositions, and visual likeness for the musical project entitled *"No More Illusions"*; and  
WHEREAS, both parties wish to collaborate exclusively to publish, market, and monetize the interactive game album experience on digital app stores and web portals;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. GRANT OF LICENSE & RIGHTS
1. **Music & Likeness License**: Artist hereby grants to Studio an exclusive, worldwide license during the Term to incorporate, synchronize, stream, package, and distribute the audio recordings (including master stems, vocal tracks, and instrumentals) and Artist's name, logo, behind-the-scenes video footage ("B-Roll"), and likeness into the Game application across Google Play, Apple App Store, CrazyGames, and associated promotional channels.
2. **Reservation of Pure Audio Rights**: Artist retains 100% of all traditional DSP streaming rights (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, physical vinyl/CD distribution, live concert performance). This Agreement pertains strictly to interactive gamified software applications.

---

### 2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS
1. **Studio Technology Ownership**: Studio owns 100% of the underlying software code, game mechanics, physics engines, UI/UX designs, algorithmic beat-mapping pipelines, shader architectures, and software patents/trade secrets embodied in the Platform.
2. **Artist IP Ownership**: Artist owns 100% of the master sound recordings, musical compositions, lyrics, and registered trademarks associated with the Artist's personal brand and band identity.
3. **Interactive Format Exclusivity**: Artist agrees not to license, develop, or authorize any competing interactive rhythm game, mobile game album, or gamified music application based on the *"No More Illusions"* tracklist with any third-party developer during the Term without Studio's prior written consent.

---

### 3. REVENUE SHARE & MONETIZATION
1. **Net Revenue Definition**: "Net Revenue" shall mean gross revenues actually received by Studio from the Game (including in-app purchases, cosmetic item sales, digital merch passes, and AdMob advertising revenue), minus store platform processing fees (Google/Apple 15–30%), chargebacks, and direct third-party hosting/CDN fees.
2. **Revenue Split**:
   * **Studio**: 50% of Net Revenue.
   * **Artist**: 50% of Net Revenue.
3. **Accounting & Payouts**: Studio shall provide quarterly accounting statements and disburse earned royalties within forty-five (45) days of the end of each calendar quarter, subject to a minimum \$50.00 payout threshold.

---

### 4. TERM & TERMINATION
1. **Term**: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial period of **two (2) years** ("Initial Term"), automatically renewing for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.
2. **Termination for Cause**: Either party may terminate upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party breaches a material obligation and fails to cure within said thirty (30) day period.

---

### 5. PROMOTION & COMMUNITY COMMITMENTS
1. **Artist Promotion**: Artist agrees to actively promote the closed beta and public launch via their official social media channels (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), including sharing download links, behind-the-scenes teasers, and recruiting initial fan beta testers.
2. **Studio Support**: Studio agrees to maintain the server infrastructure, implement ongoing performance optimizations, and provide technical store support.
