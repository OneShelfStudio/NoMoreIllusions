Privacy Policy

Application: No More Illusions: Album Game • Developer: One Shelf Studio • Effective Date: November 4, 2026
Summary: No More Illusions is an interactive musical arcade experience. We respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay progression is stored locally on your device or safely synchronized via platform-standard services.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.

  • Local Gameplay Data: High scores, unlocked tracks, customized sound-runner skins, and in-game note points are stored locally on your device using hardware-backed AES-256 encryption (EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android; localStorage / IndexedDB in web browsers).
  • Non-Personal Device Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and performance data (such as app crashes, device model, operating system version, and refresh rate) may be collected automatically by Google Play Services to ensure technical stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Monetization

The application integrates reputable, industry-standard third-party SDKs to provide monetization and store features:

  • Google AdMob & Ad Mediation (Unity Ads, Liftoff/Vungle): Provides optional rewarded video ads (e.g., revive runs) and non-intrusive banners. These services may process pseudonymous advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies. You may reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes optional purchases (e.g., full album unlock). All financial and transaction details are handled exclusively by Google Play; One Shelf Studio never sees or stores your payment details or credit card information.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

No More Illusions is designed as a general audience title for music fans of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not maintain external servers storing user accounts or personal profiles, your data remains in your control. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data will erase all locally saved game progression.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with an updated effective date.
